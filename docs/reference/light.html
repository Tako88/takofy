    Light mode

    7 decisions · 1 inversion

    Preview

    Dark is still what ships. This sheet is light because it is being read, not because the product changed, and the switch at the top right of this sheet is the real one: two buttons, one attribute on <html>, a stored word, and the whole app re-skins because nothing in it knows a colour that is not a token. No component carries a second stylesheet and no specimen below is redrawn in a lighter grey — every one of them is the app's own markup, rendered by the light values of the same names. Where the other sheets say bone, read ink: they were written in the dark theme, and this sheet is the record of what changes when the ground does.

    01The ladder does not flip

    same order · new floor

    TokenDarkLightWhy
    --color-window #000000 #e6e2d9

    The desk behind the panels. It was the darkest thing on screen and it becomes the deepest thing on screen — that is the whole trick, and it is why the 8px channels still read as seams rather than as gaps.

    --color-surface #070708 #faf9f5

    A panel. Warm paper, not white: the bone cast survives the flip, which is the reason the two themes still look like one material and not like a brand and its screenshots.

    --color-surface-raised #121215 #ffffff

    Popups. Elevation is “lighter than the ground” in both themes — this is the value that proves the ladder is not inverted, only re-based. On paper the popup is the only pure white in the interface.

    --color-surface-hover #1c1c20 #efece4

    Under the pointer, and the fill of every pressed control. One step toward the ink in light, one step toward the light in dark: both are “one step”, which is why the chips and the panel tabs needed no new rule.

    --color-panel #0b0b0d #f4f2ea

    The Now Playing pane — the one region that is neither the page nor a popup, so it sits a shade off the panel in both directions.

    --color-surface-sunken #000000 #e9e5db

    A well — it reads deeper than the window. The frame around every specimen on this page, and the reason the specimens do not bleed into the sheet.

    The mistake this section exists to prevent: treating light mode as “the same ladder, upside down”. Invert the order and popups end up darker than panels, which reads as a hole rather than as a layer, and the skeleton you see on the loading sheet stops matching the panel it predicts.

    02Ink, and the hairline problem

    16.6 : 7.4 : 6.2

    Every green room has a window

    Muted ink, at reading size on a panel. The hierarchy was built out of size, weight and the mono/roman split, so it survives the ground changing under it — the three tiers run 16.6:1, 7.4:1 and 6.2:1 against paper, 5.2:1 for the last one on the well these specimens sit in, and 17.5:1, 7.7:1 and 4.4:1 against black.

    Subtle · mono caps · 11px

    TokenDarkLightWhy
    --color-text #f2f0ea #1b1a17

    Bone becomes charcoal. Not black: #1b1a17 is the same warm ink the bone is, one end of the scale further.

    --color-text-muted #b0aca4 #55524b

    The paragraph above. Muted still has to be readable — a dim tier is a rank, not an excuse.

    --color-text-subtle #83807a #605d56

    The mono label above. 6.2:1 on a panel and 5.2:1 on the well — the darkest the type scale ever goes, and it still clears AA at 11px. The dark theme held this tier at 4.4:1; on paper a dimmer label does not read as quiet, it reads as a mistake, so the floor moved up.

    --color-border bone 11% ink 14%

    The one genuinely new arithmetic. Bone at 11% on paper is invisible, and ink at 11% on paper is a heavier line than bone at 11% on black — so the alpha steps up a little and the ink changes owner.

    --color-border-strong bone 26% ink 30%

    The edge of a pressed control. Same relationship to the hairline in both themes, so a hovered chip is a hovered chip.

    03Green, deepened

    2.7:1 → 5.9:1 · still sound only

    Why the dark green fails

    #7da566 on paper is 2.7:1. As the 2pt rule at the margin of a playing row that is survivable — a rule is a mark, not text — but as the play glyph on a cover, or the filled heart in a tracklist, it is a control drawn in a colour you have to hunt for. The accent is the one token whose light value could not simply be lifted from the dark set.

    What ships instead

    #3f6b33 — the same moss, two steps deeper, 5.9:1 on a panel. Hover goes darker rather than brighter (#35592b), because on paper emphasis is depth. Everything green already meant still means it: the sounding row's rule, the equaliser, the play tile, the focused field, the liked heart.

    04Rows, in light

    6% · 15% · 2pt

    Rest · hover · selected · selected and sounding

    1. 1 Signal Decay 4:12
    2. 2 Wintering 3:58
    3. 3 Harbour Wall 4:05
    4. 4 Low Country 4:31

    Rest, hover, selected, selected and sounding. The three claims keep the shapes they had — an area for hover, a bigger area for selection, a 2pt rule for sound — and only the ink under them changed owner: 6% and 15% charcoal instead of 6% and 16% bone. The percentages are close because they were tuned as a relationship, and the relationship is the contract: read the drop from hover to selected on paper, then flip the switch and read it again.

    05Menus, veils and shadows

    white on paper · softer lift

    • Tracks 3 of 20

      • Harbour Wall Vela Moreno · Slow Tide
      • Harbour Fog Nils Aker · Cold Storage
      • Harbour Bell The Rookery · Halcyon Street
    Menu · --shadow-lg
    0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65) → 0 18px 44px rgba(35, 30, 20, 0.16). The lift is carried by the popup's hairline and its one step of extra lightness; the shadow only has to prove contact with the page.
    Popover · --shadow-md
    0 6px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55) → 0 6px 18px rgba(35, 30, 20, 0.12).
    Control · --shadow-sm
    0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5) → 0 1px 2px rgba(35, 30, 20, 0.10). Barely there in either theme, which is the point of a small control's shadow.
    Scrim · --color-scrim
    black 78% → ink 42%. A modal needs less veil to hide paper than to hide black, and more of it reads as a grey filter laid over the page rather than as depth.

    The menu is the only surface that goes up in the light set: pure white, a hairline, and a shadow that is now a contact shadow rather than a hole in the page. The lift is carried by the border, which is why the shadow can be this quiet — a dark-theme shadow at 55% black on paper does not read as depth, it reads as soot. The scrim lightens for the same reason: 78% black over a white page is a grey filter, 42% still hides it.

    06Artwork does not invert

    3 tokens held · 2 veiled

    • Ambient
    • Indie folk
    • Post-rock
    • Dark pop

    A photograph is not a surface, so it does not get a light value. Every tint above is still the artwork's own dark colour, which is why its label is still bone (--color-on-art) in both themes and why nothing about the browse grid changed between them. What did flip is the veil: the artist hero washes its photograph with ink in dark and with paper in light, so the title underneath stays legible either way, and the play glyph that sits on a cover keeps the bright green (--color-accent-on-art) because the veil under it is the one thing that never gets lighter. The liked-songs tile likewise swaps its charcoal gradient for sand rather than for white — it is a cover, not a panel.

    07What gets built

    1 file · 0 component forks

    Ten lines of CSS, two files, and one promise about the dark theme. Nothing below is a component rule — if a component needed one, its dark rule would have been wrong.

    styles/light.css
    Every token above in one html[data-theme="light"] block, imported after the dark store. Same specificity, later in the cascade, so no !important anywhere in the file.
    theme.js
    Eleven lines in <head> that read one stored word before paint. Without it a light window flashes dark for a frame; with it the switch costs nothing on reload. ?theme=light overrides without storing, which is how each page gets reviewed.
    --color-on-art
    #f2f0ea in both themes. Ink printed on artwork or on a tint taken from artwork; it is the one ink that cannot follow the ground.
    --color-accent-on-art
    #7da566 dark · #93bb7c light. Green over a dark veil keeps its bright step for the reason given in 03 — the veil is the only surface that stays dark.
    --color-art-veil-a / -b
    ink 32% and 58% → paper 55% and 86%. The artist hero's wash, and the reason type over a photograph survives the flip.
    --color-hero-tint
    #3a3a3a → #ded9cb. The fallback band before the canvas has sampled the cover; the sampled band is re-tinted in main.js, because 16–34% lightness is a slate slab under charcoal ink.
    --color-liked-from / -to
    #1a1a20 → #eae4d6 and #33333c → #c7bfa9. The liked-songs tile is a cover, so it gets a sand gradient and the normal ink, not a white one.
    --color-scrollbar / -hover
    Bone washes → charcoal washes. Extracted from the stylesheet's literals with --color-track: a scrollbar or an unfilled track that keeps its bone value is the tell that a dark palette was pasted onto paper.
    --color-track
    rgba(242, 240, 234, 0.2) → rgba(28, 26, 22, 0.18). The unfilled half of a seek bar, a progress strip and the phone's minibar — one value for all three.
    --color-row-hover / -selected
    6% and 16% bone → 6% and 15% ink. Also extracted from three literals (4%, 5%, 8%) that had drifted apart, so every row in the app now hovers by the same amount in both themes.

    And the promise: dark did not move. Nothing in the dark theme changed except those literals collapsing into tokens, which is why this page can be checked by switching it off — if the dark sheet looks different after the light one shipped, the light one took something it did not pay for. Dark stays the default and remains the only thing a first-time reader sees.
