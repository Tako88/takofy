    Search reference

    8 decisions · static specimens

    The search input, decided. The fields on this page are real controls; the menus are static specimens of the states they produce — there is no index behind this page, so typing here will not query anything.

    01Field, in three states

    border only · 3 states

    Empty

    The placeholder names what the index actually holds. It is not a question the client cannot answer, and it is not the word “Search” echoed back at you from inside a search field.

    Focused, typing

    The border goes bone and nothing else moves: no glow, no icon swap, no width change. The input is real, so the caret is real. Results start at the first character; focus alone opens nothing.

    With text

    The clear control sits inside the field's own box, at its right edge, 4px from the border. Clearing therefore never changes the field's width — and never moves the menu that hangs off it. Escape does the same thing without the pointer.

    Same width as the field, 6px below it, 3px radius, one hairline border plus the app's largest shadow. Rows are 44px: a 36px cover, the title over its owner, and a mono kind tag at the right edge. No duration column — a duration cannot compare a four-minute track with a 128-episode show, and the kind is the thing that tells you what Enter will do. The highlighted row trades its kind tag for the action glyph: a chevron for anything that opens, play/pause for a track. At rail width the tag is the first thing to go — see section 08.

    03Highlight, hover, and the row you are hearing

    last device wins

    Active — Enter opens this

    • Harbour Lights Vela Moreno Album

    The keyboard's row: a 2px bone rule at the margin, a lit background, and the action glyph at the right edge.

    Pointer, while the keyboard holds another row

    • Harbour Wall Vela Moreno · Slow Tide Track

    A 4% wash and a 1rem tick at the margin — nothing more. It never borrows the rule or the glyph, so there is exactly one place that says “this is what Enter will fire”.

    This is the track you are hearing

    • Slow Tide Vela Moreno · Slow Tide Track

    The accent rule at the margin, and the equaliser in place of the kind tag. If the arrows land on this row as well, the accent stays — identity beats focus — and the glyph becomes pause.

    Which one wins when they disagree: the last device to move. Move the pointer and it takes the rule; press an arrow and the rule returns to the keyboard, leaving the row under the cursor as a wash and a tick. Enter always fires the ruled row. A click fires whatever it lands on, which is precisely why the pointer's row must never look selected.

    04The floor of the list

    5 rows + 1 action

    • Harbour Road Wanderline Podcast
    • See all 12 results for “harbour”

    Five results, then a sixth row that is an action: See all 12 results for “harbour”. Without that row the results page would be reachable only by pressing Enter on the right result — a keyboard-only route. It is a full 44px row in the menu's mono voice, it takes the same highlight, and it is the only row without a cover. When the total is five or fewer there is nothing left over, so the action row is not rendered.

    05Over the page

    no scrim · z 40

    Menuz 40 · escapes the top bar
    Top barstatic · no clip
    View contentscrolls under, undimmed
    App paneloverflow hidden · radius 4

    The menu is a lens, not a mode: no scrim, no blur, no dimming of the page beneath — the covers in section 02 stay at their normal brightness while five results float over them. It is not clipped by the top bar, which is static and has no overflow of its own; it crosses that hairline and is stopped only by the app panel's rounded frame. On a phone that panel is the viewport, so the viewport edge is where the list ends.

    Neither of the playlist tables as it stands. Results are of different kinds, so the Album column says nothing — a track has one, a playlist does not — and it is replaced by Kind; the artist folds under the title, which is exactly the anatomy of the menu's rows. Coming out of the menu into the page, nothing has to be re-read. No count line under the heading: the total sits in the head's right slot, where every other view keeps its size readout. And nothing where the album band would be — a query has no artwork to tint from. At rail width the Kind column drops with the menu's kind tag, leaving the cover and the title.

    20rem rail

    At rail width the field is the rail: 20rem minus the drawer control and the nav cluster. The menu follows the field exactly, the kind tag drops, and the query in the action row truncates instead of wrapping. Rows keep their 44px height — this is the one place the row loses a column, never its target size.
